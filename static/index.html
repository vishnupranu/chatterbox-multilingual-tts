Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our voice synthesis platform and permission system.

Can I synthesize complex scientific papers in Telugu and Hindi?
Yes. Our acoustic dictionary is fine-tuned for scholarly and technical terminology. When pasting thesis abstracts or research articles in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, or English, the neural model preserves technical terms while synthesizing natural academic cadence.
How does child-safe storytelling with sound effects work?
The Early Educator persona dynamically modulates vocal warmth, slows tempo for young ears, and inserts descriptive acoustic queues for animal sounds and whimsical tale settings, making learning joyful and safe.
What permissions do Super Admin and Platform Admin have?
Super Admin (Owner) holds root privileges to inspect all registered users, audit logs, system telemetry, and active worker queues. Platform Admin can monitor performance metrics and system load. General users (PG Researchers, Students, Professionals) access synthesis workspaces tailored to their tier.
Can I run synthesis locally on Apple Silicon without cloud dependencies?
Yes. The engine automatically detects Apple Silicon hardware and utilizes Metal Performance Shaders (MPS) for high-performance, low-latency 24kHz synthesis completely on-device without sending your scripts to third parties.
How does Scan to Pay UPI work for subscription plans?
Select any plan (Starter, Pro, or Enterprise) to generate an official UPI QR code with pre-filled payment parameters. Once paid via any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), submit the UTR / Ref number for automated validation.
